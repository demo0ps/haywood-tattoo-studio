Healing

Look after it, keep it sharp.

What you do in the first two weeks decides how the piece ages. You also leave every session with a printed sheet.

01Fresh tattoo

  1. Wrap stays on for the first three to five hours, then remove it gently.
  2. Wash twice a day with lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap, pat dry — never rub.
  3. Moisturise in a thin layer, two or three times a day for about two weeks. Heavy layers suffocate the skin.
  4. Leave flakes alone. Peeling pulls ink out with it.
  5. Six weeks off the pool, the sea, and the gym’s benches. Sun is the long-term enemy — factor 50 once healed.

02Fresh piercing

  1. Sterile saline once or twice a day. Clean hands before you touch anything.
  2. Do not twist or remove the jewelry, even overnight.
  3. Watch the friction: hair, phone, masks, helmet straps, pillows.
  4. No swimming for the healing window — oceans and pools are the worst offenders.
  5. Lobe: about six weeks. Cartilage: three to nine months, sometimes more.

This is general aftercare, not medical advice. Haywood does not diagnose or treat infections or reactions — if something worries you, see a clinician, then tell the studio. Stock up on saline and care kits →

Straight answers

Asked all the time.

If your question is not here, the concierge or a WhatsApp message will sort it out.

How much does a custom tattoo cost in Pretoria?＋

Custom work starts at a studio minimum of R1,800. Most medium pieces land between R2,500 and R6,000 once size, placement, detail level and colour are accounted for. The design concierge on this page gives you an indicative range from your brief; the artist confirms the final quote before a design is locked.

Do you tattoo walk-ins?＋

For tattoos, effectively no: Haywood is a private, by-appointment studio, so each session gets the whole day and no waiting room. Standard piercing work is the exception — a walk-in can sometimes be taken when a slot is open, which is most likely on a Friday. Send a brief over WhatsApp or through the concierge and you will get a reply with available dates.

How do I book a session?＋

Describe the piece, get an indicative range, then pay a deposit to hold the date. The deposit is credited to your final price. You can book a ready-to-run lookbook piece directly, or ask for one to be adapted.

Can you come to me?＋

Yes. Mobile tattoo sessions are available in Pretoria and roughly 30 km around it, with a travel fee that is currently set at R350 and confirmed in your brief before anything is booked.

How big is the deposit?＋

Thirty per cent of the quoted range, calculated from the lower end so it never surprises you. It secures your slot and comes off the final amount.

Do you do cover-ups and touch-ups?＋

Yes, subject to assessment. Send clear, straight-on photos of the existing work in daylight and the artist will tell you honestly whether it needs one session, two, or laser first.

How old do I have to be?＋

Eighteen or older with a valid South African ID or passport, checked and kept on file at your first session. There are no exceptions, including for clients with a parent or guardian.

What aftercare does a new piercing need?＋

Sterile saline once or twice a day, leave the jewelry in, keep hair, phone screens and helmets clean, and avoid swimming for the healing window. Follow the sheet you are handed at the studio. If something worries you, see a clinician — the studio does not diagnose or treat reactions.

Who owns the design afterwards?＋

The artwork remains the artist's intellectual property; your tattoo is yours on your body. Haywood asks not to have its designs copied by other studios.

Are the prices on this site final?＋

No. Every figure you see here is an indicative range produced by the brief you fill in, and any live studio special is applied to that range automatically. The confirmed quote always comes from the artist.

Which piercings do you do, and do you do surface piercings?＋

Ear (lobe and cartilage: helix, tragus, conch), nose, tongue, belly, and dermal anchors. Surface piercings are not offered — the rejection risk is not worth your skin. Jewelry is implant-grade and priced with the slot.

What are Piercing Fridays?＋

A standing weekly rate: selected nose, ear and belly piercings at R100–R150, Fridays only, between 10:00 and 15:00. Slots are capped at around 15–20 clients, so it is strictly appointment-only. Book the slot, bring ID, and the artist confirms the exact price at the chair.

Where are you, and can I just park outside?＋

Two locations: the private flagship at 296 Francis Baard Street, Pretoria Central, and a second studio at 2241 Martha Modise Street in Mabopane. Pretoria Central has on-site and wheelchair-accessible parking; Mabopane is street parking. Both take cards and mobile pay, and both run on a book — so lock the time before you travel.

Privacy

What this demo stores: your brief, reference image name, cart and booking details stay in your own browser via localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere in demo mode. The concierge keeps the conversation so the artist can read it — it does not speak for the studio, and it will not quote a final price.

When the studio goes live: your name, number, email, brief, reference images and payment status are held to book and follow up on your session, and are handled in line with POPIA. You can ask for your record to be corrected or deleted at any time on WhatsApp.

Card details are never stored here — payments run through a hosted Yoco checkout, and the studio keeps only the receipt reference.

Terms

Age: 18+ with valid ID, checked at every first session. Health: tell the artist about medication, skin conditions, allergies, pregnancy or anything else relevant before you book — the studio may ask you to wait, and that call is the artist’s to make.

Deposits: secure a slot and are credited to your final price; they are not refunded if you simply do not show, but they move with you if you give notice. Reschedule requests go straight to the artist over WhatsApp.

Designs: the artwork is the artist’s intellectual property; the tattoo on your skin is yours. Prices: every number on this site, including any live special, is indicative until the artist confirms it.
